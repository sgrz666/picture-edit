Dear Dr. Wu and Prof. Lin,

I am writing to request access to the DNA-Rendering dataset. I have signed and
attached the License Agreement.

My name is Runzhi Shangguan, an undergraduate student at the School of
Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Normal University, where I work under the
supervision of Prof. Hongwen Zhang. My research focuses on controllable human
image editing: given a source portrait of one person together with a pose
reference, I aim to synthesize that same person — identity, clothing and body
shape preserved — in the referenced pose.

The specific problem I am addressing is identity drift. In my own experiments,
strong pose conditioning consistently erodes facial identity similarity and
clothing fidelity in the generated result, and I am looking for training data
that directly supervises this trade-off. Two properties of DNA-Rendering match
this need unusually well:

First, the dataset provides genuine cross-pose supervision for the same identity
in the same outfit. I understand each participant was recorded in three outfits
with at least nine distinct motion sequences, which is exactly the pairing
structure my task requires: identity and clothing held constant while pose
changes.

Second, the green-screen capture with a refined matting pipeline (BiSeNet v2
followed by GraphCut) yields clean silhouettes. This lets me fit a SMPL-X model
offline and render the geometry control maps my model needs — surface normals,
depth, and body-part parsing — from the released camera parameters.

To keep the storage footprint manageable, I would like to request Part 1 only,
which is documented as approximately 192 GB and already includes SMPL-X models,
3D keypoints, foreground masks and 60-view RGB. This subset alone is sufficient
for a controlled feasibility study, and I will apply separately if I later need
additional parts.

The data will be used strictly for non-commercial academic research. I will not
redistribute, resell or sublicense the raw data, will keep it on access-controlled
institutional storage, and will cite the DNA-Rendering paper in any resulting
publication.

Thank you for considering my request. I am happy to provide any further
information about our institution or the planned experiments.

Best regards,

Runzhi Shangguan
Undergraduate Student
School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Normal University
E-mail: 202511081057@mail.bnu.edu.cn
Advisor: Prof. Hongwen Zhang (zhanghongwen@bnu.edu.cn)
